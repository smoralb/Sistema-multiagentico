Carnival of Rio de Janeiro

📅 Feb 28 - Mar 5, 2026
⏱️ 5 days
📍 Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

About This Event

The Carnival in Rio de Janeiro is one of the world's most spectacular celebrations, attracting millions of visitors from around the globe. This iconic festival transforms the entire city into a vibrant tapestry of music, dance, and color.

The heart of Rio Carnival is the Sambadrome parade, where samba schools compete in elaborate displays featuring thousands of dancers, musicians, and massive float displays. Each school tells a story through their theme, costumes, and choreography.
